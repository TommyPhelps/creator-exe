## CREATOR.EXE 0.1.3: YouTube and Twitch, more variety, no Let's Plays

Open it, decide whether you feel like making a video or streaming, find something interesting, and get started.

### Download
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.3_x64-setup.exe`: recommended; install over 0.1.2
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.3_x64_en-US.msi`
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.3_x64_portable.exe`
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`

Not code-signed: on the SmartScreen prompt choose **More info → Run anyway**.

### What changed
- **No Let's Plays.**
  - Every YouTube idea needs a reason to exist beyond "I played it": a strange discovery, an experiment, a mechanic, a situation, a personal observation, a question, mods. Each card says which.
  - Plain "first time", "first hour", episodic diaries and full-playthrough formats are retired. Your old reactions to them still count.
  - Ideas say how long they take to capture: some take twenty minutes, some take days.
- **YouTube / Twitch / Both** (Idea Machine and Tonight, remembered between sessions).
  - **YouTube:** ideas that make a good standalone video.
  - **Twitch:** things that work live. **Concept Stream** means there's something particular to explore. **Casual Live** means just stream: no premise, no series, nothing to promise.
  - **Both:** works live and could become a video later, if you decide it should.
- **The stream sheet.** A Twitch project's recording sheet adds:
  - mode, title and go-live note, each with a 140-character counter (title starting points you can click, then rewrite)
  - category, what you want from the session, and talking points
  - a **Before going live** checklist
  - **I'm live** and **Stream ended** buttons that log the times
  - **Mark moment**, which notes the time since you went live, plus VOD times you type in
  - a VOD link and session notes

  Nothing touches Twitch or OBS. Twitch has no API for the go-live note, so you paste it into your dashboard.
- **A YouTube video from a stream, only if you want one.** It's a separate, linked project that keeps the stream's notes and moments. The stream and the upload are tracked separately.
- **Where do I start?** A section on the recording sheet. Three quick answers (your save, what it's about, how much context is needed) give practical guidance:
  - Usually: start where it gets interesting and set it up in a sentence.
  - A short recap when context matters.
  - A new game only when the idea is about the beginning.

  It lists what viewers actually need and the few seconds of footage worth grabbing separately. You choose; it's never a script.
- **Variety.**
  - No developer or series dominates: repeats over your last 45 suggestions are pushed back, and there are no two ideas from one studio per batch. Your favourite games boost similar genres instead of only themselves.
  - Asking for a game still gets you that game.
  - A **variety line** under each batch shows games, genres and developers over your last 30 suggestions.
- **New ideas outside Bethesda, FromSoftware and CD Projekt.**
  - 22 new ideas across co-op, party and weird online games, sims, driving, sandbox, puzzle and strategy.
  - Seventeen general formats keyed to genre, for games in your library the app has no notes on. They're labelled as general and claim nothing about the game.
- **Co-op and party games** come up with **Playing: With friends**. Competitive multiplayer only appears when you ask.
- **Games tab** (Idea Machine).
  - Your REWIND library and the catalog (113 games, up from 93) in one table.
  - Filter by genre, era, never played or free, then **What could I make?**
  - **Get genres and developers from Steam** looks up your library one game at a time. Only names or Steam app IDs are sent.
  - Discovering games you don't own, through IGDB, is planned for v0.1.4 and will plug into the same place.
- New genres: racing, puzzle, JRPG, tactics, sandbox, co-op, party, platformers, shooters.
- Projects get a **For: YouTube / Twitch / Both** switch, and board cards show where a project is going.

### Your data
Schema v5 adds:
- a destination and stream link on projects (every existing project becomes YouTube, nothing else changes)
- tables for stream sessions, moments and game facts

Nothing is removed. Backups from 0.1.0 through 0.1.2 and the 0.2.0 preview restore, and v5 backups include streams and moments.
