## CREATOR.EXE 0.1.2: from idea to recording

This update is about one path: **find an idea → start a project → see exactly how to begin**. Ideas for the games you play are now written for those games, and starting one gives you a recording sheet.

### Download
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.2_x64-setup.exe`: recommended; install over 0.1.1
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.2_x64_en-US.msi`
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.2_x64_portable.exe`
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`

Not code-signed: on the SmartScreen prompt choose **More info → Run anyway**.

### What changed
- **The recording sheet.** A project now opens on one screen with:
  - the game, its year and genres, and whether you own it
  - the hook, and why someone would watch
  - **Your take**: two or three approaches to pick from (directions, not scripts)
  - where the recording starts, and your notes
  - the next step and the first five minutes
  - moments to capture as a checklist you tick off while recording
  - what you need, listed once
  - optional visual beats, which only point at situations: how they're funny is your call
  - how big it is, and why it suits you

  Every planning field from 0.1.1 is still there, under **More notes**.
- **Starting an idea keeps everything.** Game, approaches, moments, needs, ownership, why it suits you and your notes on the idea all carry over. Starting the same idea twice reopens its project.
- **Notes on ideas.** Inbox → an idea → My notes.
- **Ideas written for your games.**
  - 45 new hand-written ideas for Morrowind, itch.io horror, Mount & Blade: Warband, Skyrim and its mods, older PC RPGs (Daggerfall, Arcanum, Gothic, Might and Magic VI), Thief and immersive sims, Minecraft modpacks, Elden Ring, Dark Souls and The Witcher 3. Nine existing ideas also got game-specific detail.
  - They mix first impressions, challenges, unusual mechanics, mods, experiments, diaries and the occasional bit of research.
  - Facts were checked against UESP, the games' wikis, store pages and the modpacks' own pages.
  - Template ideas that only swapped one of these games' names in are no longer suggested. Your earlier feedback on them still counts.
  - "Is X an overlooked classic?" and "Is X still worth playing?" are gone.
- **Build around games** (Idea Machine): name one or more games and the batch is built around them, with different kinds of video for each.
  - It understands "Warband", "Mount and Blade", "modded Skyrim" (mod ideas only, including Enderal), "Minecraft modpacks" (several packs) and "itch.io horror".
  - A game the app doesn't know gets general formats that claim nothing about it, and the app says so.
- **Your playtime is respected.** A game with 2+ hours in your imported library, or one you've rated, favourited or marked as playing or completed, never gets a "first time" idea. You get return visits instead, and the app says why.
- **Feedback steers without taking over.** At most a third of a batch can come from what you've liked, and "I like this kind of video" counts for less than before.
- **Free mods and modpacks** (RLCraft, SkyFactory 4, Create: Above and Beyond, GT New Horizons, Enderal) say whether you own the game they need.
- **Four steps: Plan, Record, Edit, Publish.**
  - The board has four columns, and each card shows its exact stage.
  - A project's Step buttons move it between steps. The detailed stages still exist.
  - Control Room shows which step you're on.
- **1440p.**
  - Tonight shows where each idea starts and lists projects you could pick up instead.
  - Notifications can be clicked away.
- **Your channel, as you wrote it.** Channel DNA → Your channel shows Tamster's description exactly as written, line breaks included, plus notes on your avatar, banner and look. You can edit them.
- Smaller fixes:
  - "Outer Wilds'" instead of "Outer Wilds's"
  - Doom shows as Doom
  - Warband, Thief II and the modpacks are in the games catalog

### Your data
- **Schema v3 is a repair step.** Two different "v2" databases existed: 0.1.1's, and the 0.2.0 Phase 2 preview's. v3 adds whatever either one is missing and never drops anything, so both end up the same. Forensics notes from the preview are kept.
- **Schema v4** adds notes on ideas.
- Older projects open with every field they had.
- Backups from 0.1.0, 0.1.1 and the 0.2.0 preview all restore.

### Phase exit report (v0.1.2)
1. **Implemented**: everything above, in five passes (schema safety, recording sheet, game-specific ideas, engine fixes, steps and polish).
2. **Not in this update**:
   - Phase 2 video forensics (FFprobe, transcripts)
   - analytics
   - new navigation areas
   - anything that writes jokes, scripts or voices, or generates thumbnails
3. **Tests**: 100 node:test cases, 29 of them new for v0.1.2, plus 10 Rust tests. The new ones cover:
   - both v2 layouts and backups
   - the idea-to-project hand-off
   - hand-written idea completeness
   - a no-boilerplate lint
   - retired formats
   - build-around requests for Warband, Morrowind, modded Skyrim, modpacks and unknown games
   - playtime
   - the feedback cap
   - mod and modpack ownership
   - the four steps covering every stage
4. **Strict TypeScript, Rust compile and Windows installers**: all from the GitHub Actions run for the tagged commit.
5. **Commit and installer**: tag `v0.1.2`, assets on this release.
6. **Known limitations**:
   - Hand-written ideas cover the games you named; other games still use general templates.
   - The catalog is hand-curated (93 entries).
   - Minecraft isn't on Steam, so modpacks show "needs Minecraft" until Minecraft is in your imported library.
   - Installers are unsigned.
7. **Upgrade**: run the 0.1.2 installer over 0.1.1. On first launch it upgrades the database to schema v4 and says so.
8. **Acceptance tests**: [docs/ACCEPTANCE.md](https://github.com/TommyPhelps/creator-exe/blob/creator-exe-v1/docs/ACCEPTANCE.md#v012).
