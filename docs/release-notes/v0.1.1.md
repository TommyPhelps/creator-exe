## CREATOR.EXE 0.1.1: gaming-first recommendations

A focused update to the Idea Machine based on using 0.1.0. Gaming is now the heart of the channel, gaming-adjacent tech comes up now and then, and everything else only appears when you ask for it.

### Download
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe`: recommended; install over 0.1.0
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.1_x64_en-US.msi`
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.1.1_x64_portable.exe`
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`

Not code-signed: on the SmartScreen prompt choose **More info → Run anyway**.

### What changed
- **Content Focus** (Idea Machine, top panel). Presets: Gaming only, Gaming first (default: 85% gaming, 15% gaming-adjacent tech, 0% other), Gaming + tech, Variety channel, Explore outside my niche, or your own percentages.
  - The focus decides which ideas are eligible at all; it isn't just a score bonus. A domain set to 0% is never suggested, in any mode.
  - Shares are balanced across batches using what you were recently shown, so a batch of three doesn't need "0.45 of a tech idea".
  - When a part of the focus runs out of matching ideas, the batch is filled from another part you allow, never from outside your focus, and you're told.
  - **Explore outside my niche** is a separate button for one batch, with a banner saying so.
- **A new gaming idea library.** 68 concepts (57 gaming, 11 gaming-adjacent tech) across 15 kinds of video: first playthroughs, revisits, hidden gems, roulette, mods & modpacks, interesting challenges, mechanics experiments, play experiments, diaries, design analysis, personal retrospectives, history & mysteries, comparisons, Shorts, and "making it run". That's over 250 concrete ideas once games are filled in. The two ideas you liked keep their IDs, so your reactions to them still count.
- **A built-in games catalog** gives each game a genre and era. A game is only called free when there's a stated reason (e.g. "Bethesda released it as a free download in 2009"). Everything else is paid or unknown; nothing is guessed.
- **Familiar / Adjacent / Completely new / Surprise me** now work within gaming: games and genres you know, their neighbours, unfamiliar games and approaches, or surprises that are still gaming.
- **New controls** (More controls): genre, games you know vs. don't, game cost (free / owned or free / willing to buy), retro vs. modern, the game itself vs. history & research, gameplay vs. commentary, one-off vs. series, and "Inspired by games".
  - If too few ideas match, the least important filters are loosened first and you're told which.
- **Your game library.** Creator Memory → Game Library imports a REWIND backup, a CSV, or a plain list, only from the file you pick. Ideas then say "In your library", "Not in your imported library", "Free", or "Ownership unknown".
  - Zero budget never suggests buying a game, tool or equipment.
  - Tonight never needs a game you don't own.
  - Older REWIND backups and plain lists don't record ownership, so you're asked before anything is marked owned.
- **Feedback that learns the right thing.**
  - The options are now separate: I like this game, I like this kind of video, Like the topic but not this take, More like this, Less like this, and No videos of this type, alongside the old ones.
  - Liking a Morrowind idea makes Morrowind and that kind of video more likely, not ten copies of the same video.
  - All feedback fades over time. One slot per batch is kept for something genuinely new to you.
- **Variety.**
  - No repeated concept, game or video type within a batch.
  - Recently shown ideas, concepts and games are pushed back, so rerolls are fresh.
- **Creator Memory: Channel vs. Personal vs. Watching.** Only Channel entries steer ideas.
  - On first launch, interests that aren't about gaming are switched to Personal. They're kept, not deleted, and you can flip any back.
  - Gaming preferences from your feedback are added: games you like, older RPGs, retro PC, indie, nostalgia, modpacks, mechanics, bizarre games, gaming-related tech, and "never suggest: competitive multiplayer / endless grind".
- **Channel DNA classification fixed.**
  - Links (like `twitch.tv`) and lines repeated across most of your descriptions are ignored.
  - Generic words ("tv", "music", "build") only count in titles.
  - Each topic has a confidence level. A single description mention is a weak hint, and a video without real evidence shows as **Unknown** instead of a guess.
  - You can set a video's topics yourself.
- **Idea cards.**
  - Bigger text and more room at 1440p.
  - The card shows the game or topic, honest ownership, why it suits you, effort, what you need, and the first step. Everything else is under Details.
  - Game covers and current prices come from the Steam store, only when Steam's name matches the game exactly, labelled with the date checked. You can switch this off in Settings.

### Your data
Schema v2 adds columns and two tables; nothing existing is changed or removed. Your imported videos, Creator Memory, projects, feedback and thumbnails carry over, and backups from 0.1.0 still restore.

### Phase exit report (v0.1.1)
1. **Implemented**: everything above.
2. **Not in this update**: Phase 2 channel forensics (thumbnail/transcript/video analysis), AI modes, Rabbit Hole, Analytics.
3. **Tests**: 71 node:test cases, 17 of them new for v0.1.1. They cover:
   - Content Focus enforcement and every mode × interest combination
   - gaming defaults and excluding unrelated content
   - diversity and duplicate avoidance
   - no-purchase rules
   - channel classification
   - feedback learning
   - library import and Steam parsing
   - the v1→v2 migration with backup compatibility

   Plus 10 Rust tests.
4–6. **Strict TypeScript, Rust compile and Windows installers**: all from the GitHub Actions run for the tagged commit.
7–8. **Commit and installer**: tag `v0.1.1`, assets on this release.
9. **Known limitations**:
   - The Steam lookup was tested against recorded responses, not live from the build machine. If Steam changes its format, covers and prices simply don't appear.
   - The catalog is hand-curated (85 entries). Games outside it still work through your library, without genre or era.
   - Installers are unsigned.
10. **Upgrade**: run the 0.1.1 installer over 0.1.0. On first launch it migrates your database and splits your interests (a notice says what moved).
11. **Acceptance tests**: [docs/ACCEPTANCE.md](https://github.com/TommyPhelps/creator-exe/blob/creator-exe-v1/docs/ACCEPTANCE.md#v011).
