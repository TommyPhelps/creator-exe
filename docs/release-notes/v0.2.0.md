## CREATOR.EXE 0.2.0: Thumbnail Lab rebuilt, face cut-out, new look

Your screenshots, your face and your words, laid out the way your own thumbnails are.

### Download
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.2.0_x64-setup.exe`: recommended; install over 0.1.3
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.2.0_x64_en-US.msi`
- `CREATOR.EXE_0.2.0_x64_portable.exe` (everything except the face cut-out, which needs the installer's ONNX Runtime)
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`

Not code-signed: on the SmartScreen prompt choose **More info → Run anyway**.

### Thumbnail Lab
- **Concepts follow your own thumbnails.** Each style comes from your uploads and says which ones: reaction + caption, reaction + arrow, rules list, one big word, question, error box. 2018 WordArt, tilted banner and split screen are offered when you tick **Include experimental**.
- **Built on real material only.** Concepts use your screenshots and your cut-out. With no screenshot you get no concepts, never words on a coloured box.
- **Checked before you see them.** Text is fitted with the bundled fonts. Any concept with text over your face, text over text, an arrow over words, or words under YouTube's video-length corner is dropped. If a style needs more words (one big word, a question, an error box program), the Lab says so.
- **No facecam needed.** "Words over gameplay" uses no face, and the arrow and question styles work without one. With no cut-out you still get three concepts (experimental styles fill in, and are labelled).
- **Inspired by:** pick one of your thumbnails and its style comes first.
- **Soft images are flagged.** If a screenshot or photo has to be enlarged more than 1.6×, Checks say it will look soft.
- **Libraries:** screenshots and cut-outs you add are kept for next time.

### Face cut-out (on this PC)
- **Cut out a photo** cuts you out of a photo or webcam frame with a white sticker outline, like your thumbnails.
- You see every result before it's kept. The model keeps the main subject of a photo; given a photo with nobody in it, it can keep an object instead, so you choose Keep or Discard.
- It runs on your PC with U²-Net human segmentation (Apache-2.0) and ONNX Runtime 1.31 (MIT). The first time, it asks before a one-time 176 MB model download from the rembg project's GitHub release, then checks the file's SHA-256.
- Nothing is uploaded.

### Editor
- Layers with show/hide/lock, drag, resize, rotate, crop, undo/redo.
- Grouped layers (like the error box) move together.
- **Align** buttons, **Checks** that update as you work and are marked on the canvas, plus a safe area and the video-length corner.
- Sticker outline and brightness/colour for images; gradient and bent text; curved arrows.
- **Preview on YouTube**: dark and light home feed, plus the 168 px Up next size.
- **Export** to PNG (1280×720), **PSD with layers** (opens in Affinity Photo, Photoshop, GIMP, Krita; text arrives as pixels), SVG, OpenRaster and .cthumb. The fonts are embedded in PNG, PSD and SVG exports.

### New look
- Near-black, flat and square, with thin borders and one accent colour (Settings → Accent colour). No more title-bar gradients, bevels or green terminal text.
- The section list and the Lab/editor panels can be resized by dragging their edges.
- Idea cards all have the same header. **Show cover art on idea cards** turns the store art off.

### Data
- Schema v6 adds the Thumbnail Lab library (additive). Thumbnails made in 0.1.x open and save unchanged.

### What was tested
- 146 automated tests, including:
  - concepts with and without a face, from 8 image sizes (320×180 up to 7680×4320, portrait and ultrawide), 4 cut-out shapes and 5 captions: no overlaps, nothing off-frame or under the video length
  - nothing invented; old 0.1.x thumbnails still open; a 0.1.3 database upgrades with every thumbnail and project unchanged
  - the PSD writer (read back with Pillow and ImageMagick)
- Windows build: Rust tests include a real cut-out run with the bundled ONNX Runtime and the downloaded model (checksum verified); installers built.
- The interface was driven end to end at 2560×1440: add screenshots, cut out photos (including one with nobody in it), build concepts, edit, export PNG / PSD / SVG / OpenRaster / .cthumb, reopen a 0.1.3 thumbnail and export it. The exported files were checked: PSD layer names and order match the thumbnail, its flattened image is identical to the PNG, the OpenRaster file is valid.

### Still to try with your own images
- The test images were small crops (about 200 px tall) from your channel's thumbnails, so they only prove layout, cut-out and export, not final sharpness. Full-size screenshots and photos imported in the app are the real test.
- The face cut-out was run on Windows with a synthetic test image, and on your thumbnail crops with the same model elsewhere. The first real run on your PC also does the one-time model download.
- Drag-and-drop into the editor, and very large photos (phone photos over 12 MP), haven't been tried on Windows.
